Chicken Trail Designer

Build

Click a cube face to add the next cube against it, or click the ground to start one. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Shift-click inspects a cube. Coop door marks where the trail meets the coop — it drives the reach check, not the parts list; a real gate panel is a face override on the selected cube. Keys: 1-4 ground level, q/e orbit, f frame all, b/x/s/d tools, c connectors, h roof, z undo.

Per is how many pieces of hardware one joined edge eats — a 90° corner takes two, a straight joiner one. Untracked parts are still tallied but never limit a build.
